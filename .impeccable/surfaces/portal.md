## Direction

The audience is a pharmacy purchaser at a desktop during a replenishment round and a distribution administrator reviewing exceptions. The product should read as a working procurement portal immediately. The design source is the restrained graphic language of a distribution network's printed service guide: ink-navy identity, forest and eucalyptus accents, lucid humanist type, light table rules, compact labels.

Grounded list, in order: clinical reference publisher; modern pharmacy identity; healthcare campus wayfinding; wholesale product catalog; distribution service guide; apothecary package typography; public-health report.

The fifth candidate supplies type, palette, density and one signature: the connected fulfillment strip that marks the current stop from Submitted to Delivered. Standard web navigation, forms and data tables retain familiar behavior. White work surfaces and a pale cool ground support prolonged daytime reading; the navy rail holds stable navigation. IBM Plex Sans is self-hosted.

Challenger judgments: variable type specimen and Crouwel grid lose task clarity; their contribution is strict numerical alignment and consistent spacing. Botanical folio loses procurement recognition; retain its discipline of stable scales across status stages. Convention catalog loses scanning clarity; retain compact but readable information density. Transit diagram is competitive in progress states but loses audience identification; the distribution service guide remains the chosen system, with the progress strip carrying a clear current stage.

## Surface contract

Dashboard first viewport: persistent rail, pharmacy identity, cart, primary Create an order action, a compact four-column summary strip, recent-order table and inventory exceptions. Each metric and exception comes from the API. Catalog: filter row, count, table, pack quantities, add buttons. Order detail: fulfillment strip, itemized pricing, chronological history and constrained actions. Login: product explanation and a conventional two-role sign-in form.

At 390px, navigation moves above content; dense tables scroll inside labeled regions. Every input has a label, every state has text, and every action has a loading/disabled path. Native focus, dialogs and validation remain available. Motion is limited to notification arrival and respects reduced motion. No images are needed to operate this product.

Synthetic data is identified in the interface. There is no claim of regulatory compliance or distributor affiliation.
